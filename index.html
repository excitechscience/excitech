FAQs

Which classes do you offer programmes for?

Plus one and plus two science (physics, maths, & chemistry) tuition and entrance foundation course for Classes 8–10.

Do you prepare students for entrance exams?

Yes. Our Plus One and Plus Two regular tuition is integrated with entrance foundation training, helping students build strong concepts while preparing for JEE, NEET, KEAM, IISER Aptitude Test, CUET and NEST. We also offer dedicated foundation programmes for Classes 8–10.

Do you offer career guidance?

Yes. We provide personalised course and career guidance for students and parents, helping them explore suitable academic pathways, understand career opportunities and make informed decisions about their future.

How can I enrol at ExciTech Science Academy?
