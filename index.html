Frequently asked questions

Which classes do you teach?

Plus One and Plus Two science tuition, and an entrance foundation programme for Classes 8–10.

Do you prepare students for entrance exams?

Yes. We prepare students for NEET, JEE, KEAM and IISER.

Do you help with course and career choices?

Yes. We guide students on course selection, scholarships, higher education and career planning.

How is your teaching different?

We focus on concepts first, with personalised teaching, strong scientific foundations and regular problem-solving practice.
